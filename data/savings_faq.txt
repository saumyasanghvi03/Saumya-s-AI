Question: What is a high-yield savings account?
Answer: A high-yield savings account is a type of savings account that typically offers a higher interest rate than a traditional savings account. This means your money can grow faster over time. They are usually offered by online banks and credit unions.

Question: Why should I save money?
Answer: Saving money is important for several reasons. It provides financial security for emergencies, helps you reach financial goals like buying a house or retiring, and can reduce financial stress.

Question: How much should I save each month?
Answer: The amount you should save depends on your income, expenses, and financial goals. A common guideline is to save at least 20% of your income, often referred to as the 50/30/20 rule (50% for needs, 30% for wants, 20% for savings). However, even small amounts saved consistently can make a big difference.
